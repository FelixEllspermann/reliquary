# Artwork-Prompts — 5 Archetypes (50 Karten, 0.1.7)

*Gleicher Stil-Block wie ARTWORK-PROMPTS-ROAD-TO-1000.md. Zusätzlich je
Archetyp eine Farb-/Stimmungsklammer — Karten einer Familie sollen als
Familie lesbar sein.*

**STIL-BLOCK (vor jeden Prompt):**
> Dark fantasy trading card game illustration, painterly oil painting style,
> dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, rich muted palette with gold accents,
> intricate detail, no text, no borders, no watermark, centered composition.

**Familien-Klammern (nach dem Stil-Block anhängen):**
- Giftwyrm: *sickly sweet palette of ribbon-pink, poison-green and black; gift wrap, silk ribbons, chitin.*
- Splithoof: *ember-red and brass; contract paper, candle smoke, cloven hooves, sly grins.*
- Waylay: *moonlit forest greens and highway dust; hedgerows, lanterns, tricorne silhouettes.*
- Bylaw: *bureaucratic beige, ink-black and seal-red; stamps, ledgers, marble halls.*
- Chimekeep: *ivory, brass and midnight blue; clockwork, bells, glowing hour markers.*

---

## Giftwyrm (DARK / Animal)

1. **Giftwyrm Ribboncoil** — A sleek black worm coiled into a perfect bow shape atop a beautifully wrapped gift box, one glowing eye peeking from the knot, the wrapping paper subtly pulsing.
2. **Giftwyrm Sweettooth** — A plump grinning worm with tiny teeth gnawing a heart-shaped hole through a chocolate box from inside, crumbs and glitter, mana wisps escaping toward the viewer.
3. **Giftwyrm Prettybow** — An oversized luxurious silk bow on a small box — looking closer, the entire bow is a segmented worm with dozens of tiny legs holding the pose.
4. **Giftwyrm Unboxed** — A gift box torn open from within, wrapping shredded, a fanged worm mid-lunge toward the viewer, ribbon still caught on its horn, party confetti falling.
5. **Giftwyrm Registry** — A leather-bound gift registry whose entries are wriggling worm silhouettes, a quill made from a worm writing a new name, wax seals shaped like larvae.
6. **Giftwyrm Thank-You Note** — An elegant thank-you card on a doormat, the handwriting formed by tiny worm trails, a slime-glistening ribbon bow, ominous shadow under the door.
7. **Giftwyrm Molting** — Several split-open worm skins arranged like discarded gift wrap, glowing newly-hatched forms slithering away in formation, shed ribbons everywhere.
8. **Giftwyrm Hollowshell** — A hollow chitinous shell shaped like a gift box, cracked open and empty, faint green afterglow inside, wilted ribbon, viewer-level dread.
9. **Giftwyrm, Ungrateful Guest** — A refined worm in a tiny dinner jacket lounging across an entire banquet table, feet on the host's plate, presenting a suspiciously ticking gift.
10. **Giftwyrm, the Whole Hamper** — A giant picnic hamper bursting at the seams, dozens of ribboned worms erupting outward in a fountain, the largest wearing the hamper lid as a crown.

## Splithoof (FIRE / Demon)

11. **Splithoof Doorknocker** — A dapper devil with polished cloven hooves knocking on a cottage door, clipboard under arm, salesman's smile, contract pages glowing faintly in his case.
12. **Splithoof Pennyweight** — A small imp perched on one pan of a merchant's scale, pressing a thumb on the other pan, innocent whistle, coins levitating mid-cheat.
13. **Splithoof Grinning Ledger** — A thick leather ledger with a literal toothy grin along its spine, coins and cards being fed between its page-teeth, brass clasps like cufflinks.
14. **Splithoof Hagglehorn** — A ram-horned demon merchant leaning over a market stall, horns wrapped in price tags, holding up two fingers with two entirely different deals written in smoke.
15. **Splithoof Sign Here** — A devil's clawed hand offering an ornate fountain pen across a contract, the dotted line literally burning, the pen dripping something too red for ink.
16. **Splithoof Repossession** — Chained devils carrying away a knight's warhorse, armor and shadow while he still stands in heroic pose unaware, repossession tags fluttering.
17. **Splithoof Fiddle Wager** — A devil and a duelist mid-fiddle-contest on a crossroads at dusk, bows trailing fire and gold light respectively, sheet music burning between them.
18. **Splithoof Collections Imp** — A tiny imp in a bowler hat climbing through a window with an enormous ledger strapped to its back, holding an overdue notice like a dagger.
19. **Splithoof, Fair and Square** — A tall devil in an immaculate suit holding perfectly balanced scales at eye level, halo of contract clauses orbiting his horns, both pans holding handshakes.
20. **Splithoof, the Better Bargain** — A massive suave archdevil leaning back in a leather chair behind a desk of obsidian, three quill pens signing three contracts at once, brimstone chandeliers.

## Waylay (WIND / Human)

21. **Waylay Hedgeknife** — A wiry figure exploding out of a hedgerow in a shower of leaves, twin hedge-trimming blades crossed, startled birds, motion streaks.
22. **Waylay Roadthorn** — A crouched ambusher covered in a thorn-branch cloak blending into roadside brambles, only eyes and a caltrop-studded gauntlet visible.
23. **Waylay Tollgate** — A rickety toll gate on a forest road manned by grinning silhouettes in the trees, a lockbox overflowing with coins, sign with a coin symbol.
24. **Waylay Highwayman** — A classic tricorne-hatted highwayman on a rearing horse blocking a moonlit road, coat billowing, flintlock raised skyward, carriage lanterns reflecting in his grin.
25. **Stand and Deliver!** — Point-of-view of a carriage passenger: a masked figure filling the carriage window, gloved hand outstretched, moonlight rim-lighting the tricorne, breath fog.
26. **Waylay Mislead** — A wooden signpost at a forest fork with all arrows twisted to point the same wrong way, fresh sawdust, a shadowy figure adding one more nail, lantern on the ground.
27. **Waylay Campfire Fence** — A jovial fence at a hidden campfire weighing stolen goods, blankets covered in candlesticks, rings and a suspiciously familiar crown, firelight on trees.
28. **Waylay Nightparcel** — An enormous burlap-wrapped parcel standing upright on a midnight road, rope straining, two boots visible beneath it, a delivery tag reading only a skull.
29. **Waylay, First Out of the Bushes** — A lean captain of bandits mid-leap from the treeline ahead of his gang, first light catching his blade, the others still shadows in the leaves behind.
30. **Waylay, King of the Crossroads** — A regal bandit king seated on a throne built from signposts and toll gates at a five-way crossroads, roads radiating like a crown, subjects lurking in every ditch.

## Bylaw (EARTH / Human)

31. **Bylaw Filing Clerk** — A diminutive clerk atop a wheeled ladder before an infinite wall of filing cabinets, one glowing drawer open, papers orbiting her like moths.
32. **Bylaw: Quiet Hours** — A colossal marble tablet law hanging over a battlefield, engraved with a bell in a crossed-out circle, soldiers below tiptoeing with weapons wrapped in cloth.
33. **Bylaw: Show of Hands** — A courtroom where every person's hand of playing cards floats above their head fully visible, mirrored ceiling, a judge's gavel casting a long shadow.
34. **Bylaw: Standing Room Only** — A velvet rope across a grand hall entrance, exactly three ornate pedestals inside, a fourth monster waiting outside with a paper number ticket.
35. **Bylaw Enforcer** — A broad bailiff in a sash of official seals, his cudgel a rolled-up legal scroll thick as a tree trunk, standing before a wall of glowing decrees.
36. **Bylaw Ombudsman** — A calm robed mediator holding up a single white glove that dims every glowing decree tablet around him, complainants queueing behind.
37. **Bylaw Loophole** — A tiny figure slipping through a keyhole-shaped gap in a giant wall of legal text, paragraphs bending around the hole, briefcase in hand.
38. **Bylaw Red Tape** — A knight completely mummified in red ribbon-tape, mid-charge but frozen, stamps and forms stuck to his armor, a clerk adding one more layer.
39. **Bylaw, Acting Chairwoman** — A commanding woman at a podium three sizes too big for the empty council hall, gavel raised, her shadow cast as a full assembly behind her.
40. **Bylaw, the Letter of the Law** — A towering humanoid figure formed from folded legal documents and wax seals, a paragraph symbol for a face, quill-sword at rest, marble hall.

## Chimekeep (LIGHT / Mecha)

41. **Chimekeep Windup Chorister** — A brass choirboy automaton with a large windup key in its back mid-turn, mouth open in frozen song, sheet music of gears, warm workshop light.
42. **Chimekeep Bellringer** — A stocky clockwork monk hauling a bell rope, his own head a small bell swinging opposite the great bell above, motion lines of sound.
43. **Chimekeep Pendulum** — A slender automaton hanging upside-down as the living pendulum of a cathedral clock, arms folded, hair of golden chains swinging, gears framing the scene.
44. **Chimekeep Escapement** — A close-up of an ornate escapement mechanism with a tiny robed figure caught mid-step between its teeth, each tick releasing a mote of light.
45. **Chimekeep Curfew Bell** — A great bell tower at dusk with the bell mid-swing, glowing shockwave ring pushing tiny monsters and carts out of town gates, three glowing marks fading on the bell.
46. **Chimekeep Overwind** — A panicked clockmaker cranking a windup key so hard the spring bulges through the casing, gears shedding sparks, hands of the clock spinning into a blur.
47. **Chimekeep Chime In** — A small bell automaton joyfully ringing itself in perfect sync with a giant bell's stroke, sound waves interlocking as golden rings, confetti of cogs.
48. **Chimekeep Night Round** — A lantern-headed clockwork watchman on his rounds through sleeping streets, three glowing hour marks orbiting his lantern, a second silhouette forming in the fog behind him.
49. **Chimekeep, Quarter Past Doom** — An elegant clock-knight whose breastplate clock reads a quarter past twelve, drawing a sword shaped like a clock hand, cracks of light across his armor.
50. **Chimekeep, the Midnight Carillon** — A cathedral-sized carillon machine with dozens of bells all striking at once, shockwaves visible as golden rings, tiny automatons conducting, full moon exactly at zenith.
