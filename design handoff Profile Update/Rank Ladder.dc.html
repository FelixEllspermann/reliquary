PROMOTION Below Gold Seal a win adds 20–25 RP and a loss takes only 15–20, so climbing is faster than falling. From Gold Seal upward it is flat: +25 for a win, −25 for a loss. Crossing a sub-rank threshold promotes you immediately; there is no series to play. You cannot fall out of a rank once you enter it — Gold Seal I is a floor. Within a rank, sub-ranks can be lost. Vault Seal has no RP ceiling. Its five sub-ranks are cut by leaderboard position instead: V is the top 100, I is everyone above 4 500 RP.
