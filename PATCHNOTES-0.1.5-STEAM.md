# RELIQUARY 0.1.5 — Watch This

*Patch notes for the Steam announcement — same content as the in-game NEWS panel.*

## Online duels, fixed
- Private lobbies and quick matches could open on a dead board: the coin toss and first-turn choice arrived while the MATCH FOUND popup was still up — and were lost. They are buffered now; the duel always starts.
- Private lobbies play the deck you actually selected (they quietly used your first deck before).
- Screen shake on attacks and destroyed cards is back — it was aimed at a layer nobody could see.

## 48 new cards · six archetypes
- **Deckay** — mill your own deck and profit on the way down
- **Deathpoem** — every fallen verse makes the next one heavier
- **Apocrypha** — counter-magic that silences the rest of the chain
- **Gaslight** — hand your opponent worthless illusions, then collect
- **Slowburn** — set spells to charge, detonate them a turn later
- **Mimicrypt** — copy whatever your opponent liked best

## Watch & statistics
- **WATCH tab** in Play: spectate live duels as they happen.
- The **profile** is a full page now: lifetime record, match history, live games — and a showcase of up to three slowly turning cards, in the finish you own.
- **STATISTICS tab** beside the Shop: per-card win rates and their most frequent deck partners — online matches only.

## Deck Builder
- NEW filter and golden NEW badges on freshly obtained cards — click a card and its badge retires.

## On the board
- Deck piles sit on the field and draws fly from them; milled cards linger face-up a beat longer.
- The battle phase no longer skips ahead on its own.
- Graveyard and banishment cards activate with a click in their pile.
