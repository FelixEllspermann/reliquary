# Design: 5 neue Archetypes (August 2026) — zur Freigabe

Jede Karte mit Basis-Effekt, ggf. **Passiv**, **[Infused]** (Standalone) oder
**„Or Infused"** (Coupled = Upgrade des Basis-Effekts gegen Mehrpreis).
Jeder Archetype bekommt **2 Reliquaries** (1 Legendary + 1 Rare).
Alle In-Game-Texte Englisch; Burn bleibt sparsam.

---

# 1. Deathpoem — die letzten Verse (FIRE/Human · Samurai)

Samurai schrieben vor dem Tod ihr Jisei, das Todesgedicht. Hier IST das
Selbstopfer der Effekt: Jeder Deathpoem fällt von eigener Hand und reißt
dabei etwas mit. Engine-Aufwand: **null** (alles Bestand).

**Deathpoem Initiate** — Common · LV 1 · 800/400
- Ignition (1 Mana): *Tribute this card: destroy 1 monster with 1500 or less ATK.*
- **Or Infused (3):** *destroy 1 monster instead (no ATK limit).*

**Deathpoem Duelist** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1400/200
- Ignition (2 Mana): *Tribute this card: destroy 1 monster.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Graveyard-Ignition: *Banish this card: Special Summon 1 Level 1 "Deathpoem" monster from your Graveyard.*

**Deathpoem Calligrapher** — Uncommon · LV 1 · 600/600
- **Passiv (Aura):** *Other "Deathpoem" monsters you control gain 200 ATK.*
- Trigger: *If this card is sent from the field to the Graveyard: add 1 "Deathpoem" card from your Deck to your hand.*

**Deathpoem Housebane** — Rare · LV 3 · 2000/1000
- Ignition (3 Mana): *Tribute this card: destroy up to 2 monsters.*
- **Or Infused (5):** *destroy up to 2 monsters AND 1 Spell/Artifact on the field.*

**Deathpoem Vow** — Common · Spell (1 Mana)
- *Special Summon 1 "Deathpoem" monster from your Graveyard.*
- **Or Infused (3):** *Special Summon 2 instead.*

**Deathpoem, the Final Verse** — Rare · LV 3 · 1800/1600
- Beschwörungs-Bedingung: *While you have 3+ "Deathpoem" monsters in your Graveyard: You can Special Summon this card from your hand.*
- Ignition (2 Mana): *Tribute this card: destroy 1 monster and 1 Artifact.*

**RELIQUARY · Deathpoem, the Hundredth Stanza** — Legendary · LV 3 · 2400/1800
- Summon: *5+ monsters in your Graveyard, pay 3 Mana.*
- On Summon: *You can destroy up to 2 monsters your opponent controls.*
- Ignition (3 Mana): *Tribute this card: destroy up to 3 cards on the field.* (Das hundertste Gedicht beendet das Buch.)

**RELIQUARY · Deathpoem, Unsigned Verse** — Rare · LV 2 · 1700/1500
- Summon: *Your Graveyard holds 6+ cards, pay 2 Mana.*
- **Passiv:** *Gains 100 ATK for each monster in your Graveyard.* (wächst mit jedem gefallenen Vers)
- **[Infused – 2]** Quick: *This card cannot be destroyed this turn.*

# 2. Slowburn — die lange Lunte (FIRE/Human · Magier)

Quick-Spells werden GESETZT und schwelen: sofort gezündet schwach — liegt
die Lunte eine volle Runde, zündet in deiner Standby Phase automatisch die
**geladene** Version. Engine-Aufwand: **klein** (1 neuer Pflicht-Trigger
„Charged": feuert in deiner Standby, wenn die Karte vor diesem Zug gesetzt
wurde).

**Slowburn Candlewick** — Common · LV 1 · 600/800
- Trigger: *When this card is Summoned: you can set 1 "Slowburn" Spell from your Deck.*
- **Or Infused (2):** *set 2 with different names instead.*

**Slowburn Chandler** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1000/1200
- Ignition (1 Mana): *Return 1 "Slowburn" card from your Graveyard to your hand.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Quick: *Your opponent cannot Special Summon this turn.* (Der Kerzenzieher drückt die fremde Flamme aus.)

**Slowburn Pyrekeeper** — Rare · LV 3 · 1700/1300 · **der Detonator**
- **Passiv (Aura):** *Other "Slowburn" monsters you control gain 200 DEF.*
- Ignition (3 Mana, once per turn): ***Detonate: trigger the CHARGED effect of 1 of your set "Slowburn" Spells that was set before this turn.*** (Die Lunte kurzschließen — der Kern-Move des Decks.)

**Slowburn: Tripwire** — Common · Quick Spell
- Sofort (1 Mana): *Switch 1 opposing monster to Defense Position.*
- **Geladen (auto in deiner Standby):** *Switch ALL your opponent's monsters to Defense Position.* (Die Feuerlinie zwingt das ganze Feld in Deckung.)

**Slowburn: Banked Flame** — Uncommon · Quick Spell
- Sofort (1 Mana): *Draw 1 card.*
- **Geladen:** *Draw 2 cards and gain 2 Mana this turn.* (Die Explosionsrunde.)

**Slowburn: Deep Coals** — Rare · Quick Spell
- Sofort (1 Mana): *1 opposing monster loses 400 ATK.*
- **Geladen:** *Destroy up to 2 monsters your opponent controls.*

**RELIQUARY · Slowburn, the Patient Flame** — Legendary · LV 3 · 2300/1900
- Summon: *2+ Spells in your Graveyard, pay 3 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Set up to 2 "Slowburn" Spells from your Deck.*
- Ignition (2 Mana, once per turn): ***Detonate*** *(wie Pyrekeeper, aber günstiger — der Boss zündet selbst).*

**RELIQUARY · Slowburn, Backdraft** — Rare · LV 2 · 1800/1400
- Summon: *Your opponent controls more monsters than you, pay 2 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Gain 2 Mana this turn.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Quick: *1 opposing monster loses 600 ATK.* (Die Stichflamme aus der Tür.)

# 3. Apocrypha — die verworfenen Mythen (LIGHT/Myth)

Sphinx, Hydra, Chimera — Mythen, die aus den Büchern gestrichen wurden.
Der Endboss reißt die komplette Kette unter sich aus dem Buch.
Engine-Aufwand: **mittel** (1 neuer Baustein „NegateRestOfChain": alle noch
offenen äußeren Kettenglieder lösen nicht mehr auf).

**Apocrypha Roc** — Common · LV 1 · 900/500
- Ignition (1 Mana): *Return 1 monster on the field to its owner's hand.*
- **Or Infused (3):** *return up to 2 instead.*

**Apocrypha Sphinx** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1200/1200
- Trigger: *When this card is Summoned: look at your opponent's hand and choose 1 card; they discard it.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Graveyard-Ignition: *Banish this card: look at your opponent's hand and choose 1 card; they discard it.* (Das Rätsel wird zweimal gestellt.)

**Apocrypha Chimera** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1300/900
- Quick (1 Mana): *Copy the ATK/DEF of 1 monster on the field until the end of the turn.*
- **Or Infused (2):** *copy, and this card may attack once more this Battle Phase.*

**Apocrypha Hydra** — Rare · LV 3 · 1800/1200
- Trigger: *When this card is destroyed: you can Special Summon 1 "Apocrypha" monster from your Graveyard.*
- **Or Infused (2):** *Special Summon 2 instead.* (Zwei Köpfe wachsen nach.)

**Apocrypha Fable** — Common · Spell (1 Mana)
- *Add 1 "Apocrypha" card from your Deck to your hand.*

**Apocrypha Cartographer** — Rare · LV 2 · 1100/1400 *(neu — der Kartograph verlorener Orte)*
- **Passiv:** *Gains 200 ATK for each of your banished monsters.* (Er zeichnet, was nicht mehr existiert.)
- Ignition (2 Mana): *Banish 1 card from your Graveyard: draw 1 card.*

**RELIQUARY · Apocrypha, the Unwritten** — Legendary · LV 3 · 2500/2100
- Summon: *3+ monsters in your Graveyard and 4+ Mana available, pay 2 Mana.*
- Quick (2 Mana, once per turn): ***Negate all previous links of the current chain.*** (Das letzte Wort reißt alle Seiten davor heraus.)

**RELIQUARY · Apocrypha, Torn Colophon** — Rare · LV 2 · 1900/1700
- Summon: *Your opponent controls 2+ monsters, pay 2 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Return 1 card on the field to its owner's hand.*
- **Passiv (Taunt):** *Your opponent's monsters must attack this card.* (Die fehlende Schlussseite — alle wollen sie.)

# 4. Gaslight — das eingeredete Feld (DARK/Myth · Illusionen)

0/0-„Illusion"-Tokens werden auf die GEGNERSEITE beschworen: Sie verstopfen
seine Slots, und deine Karten ernten sie. Engine-Aufwand: **am größten**
(Token-System: Beschwörung auf Gegner-Slots; Tokens verschwinden statt in
den Friedhof zu gehen und zählen für keine Friedhofs-Bedingung).

**Gaslight Lanternist** — Common · LV 1 · 700/700
- Trigger: *When this card is Summoned: Summon 1 "Illusion" Token (0/0) to your opponent's field.*
- **Or Infused (2):** *Summon 2 instead.*

**Gaslight Usher** — Common · Spell (1 Mana)
- *Summon 2 "Illusion" Tokens (0/0) to your opponent's field.*

**Gaslight Mesmer** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1100/900
- Ignition (1 Mana): *Destroy 1 "Illusion" Token your opponent controls: draw 1 card.*
- **Or Infused (2):** *destroy up to 2 and draw 1 for each.*

**Gaslight Mirrorwalk** — Uncommon · Quick Spell (1 Mana)
- *When your opponent Summons a monster: Summon 1 "Illusion" Token to their field.* (Und noch eins. Erinnerst du dich nicht?)

**Gaslight Charlatan** — Rare · LV 3 · 1600/1200
- **Passiv:** *Gains 300 ATK for each "Illusion" Token your opponent controls.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Quick: *This card cannot be targeted this turn.*

**Gaslight Curtain Call** — Rare · Spell (2 Mana)
- *Destroy all "Illusion" Tokens; draw 1 card for each (max 3).*

**RELIQUARY · Gaslight, the Grand Premiere** — Legendary · LV 3 · 2200/2000
- Summon: *Your opponent controls 3+ monsters (Tokens zählen), pay 3 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Fill your opponent's empty monster zones with "Illusion" Tokens.* (Volles Haus zur Premiere.)
- Ignition (3 Mana): *Destroy all "Illusion" Tokens; 1 opposing monster loses 400 ATK for each destroyed.*

**RELIQUARY · Gaslight, Standing Ovation** — Rare · LV 2 · 1700/1600
- Summon: *Your opponent controls at least 1 "Illusion" Token, pay 2 Mana.*
- **Passiv (Aura):** *Other "Gaslight" monsters you control gain 300 ATK.*
- On Summon: *Summon 1 "Illusion" Token to your opponent's field.*

# 5. Mimicrypt — die Krypta der Nachahmer (DARK/Demon)

Nachahmer, die Effekte aus dem GEGNERISCHEN Friedhof stehlen. Engine-
Aufwand: **mittel** (1 Kopier-Baustein: „führe den Auflösungsteil eines
Zauber-Effekts aus dem Gegner-Friedhof aus"; ggf. Gegner-Grave-Zielart).

**Mimicrypt Ghoul** — Common · LV 1 · 600/900
- Trigger: *When this card is Summoned: banish 1 card from your opponent's Graveyard.*
- **Or Infused (1):** *banish 2 instead.*

**Mimicrypt Understudy** — Uncommon · LV 2 · 1000/1000 · **der einzige Spell-Kopierer**
- Ignition (2 Mana, once per turn): *Choose 1 Spell in your opponent's Graveyard: resolve its effect as if it were yours.*

**Mimicrypt Forgery** — Uncommon · Quick Spell (1 Mana)
- *1 monster you control copies the ATK/DEF of 1 opposing monster until the end of the turn.*

**Mimicrypt Siphon** — Common · Spell (1 Mana)
- *1 opposing monster loses 400 ATK; 1 monster you control gains 400 ATK.* (Nicht kopiert — abgesaugt.)

**Mimicrypt Archivist** — Rare · LV 3 · 1500/1500 · **der Entleiher**
- **Passiv (Aura):** *Other "Mimicrypt" monsters you control gain 200 DEF.* (Die Krypta schützt ihre Kopisten.)
- Ignition (3 Mana, once per turn): ***Take control of 1 opposing monster until the End Phase.*** (Der Archivar entnimmt das Original — nur zur Ansicht, versteht sich.)

**Mimicrypt Encore** — Rare · Spell (2 Mana)
- *Special Summon a copy of 1 monster your opponent controls to your field. It vanishes during the End Phase.* (Encore! Die Vorstellung wird wiederholt — auf deiner Bühne.)

**RELIQUARY · Mimicrypt, the Borrowed King** — Legendary · LV 3 · 1000/1000
- Summon: *Your opponent's Graveyard holds 8+ cards, pay 3 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Copy the ATK/DEF of the strongest monster on the field until the end of the turn.*
- Ignition (3 Mana, once per turn): ***Special Summon 1 monster from your OPPONENT's Graveyard to your field.*** (Der König befiehlt auch fremde Tote.)

**RELIQUARY · Mimicrypt, Palimpsest** — Rare · LV 2 · 1600/1600
- Summon: *6+ cards in your opponent's Graveyard, pay 2 Mana.*
- On Summon: *Banish up to 2 cards from your opponent's Graveyard.*
- **[Infused – 2]** Quick: *Copy the ATK/DEF of 1 monster on the field until the end of the turn.* (Überschriebenes Manuskript — darunter ein fremder Text.)

## Umfang & Reihenfolge

40 Karten gesamt (30 Main + 10 Reliquaries). Engine-Bausteine: Charged-
Trigger + Detonate (Slowburn), NegateRestOfChain (Apocrypha), Token-System
(Gaslight), Friedhofs-Kopie + Gegner-Grave-Beschwörung (Mimicrypt).
Baureihenfolge nach Aufwand:
**Deathpoem → Slowburn → Apocrypha → Mimicrypt → Gaslight** — je Archetype
mit Bot-Proben, Test-Deploy und Amuse-Prompts.
