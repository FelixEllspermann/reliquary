0.1.1 · FIVE NEW WAYS TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────

61 NEW CARDS, FIVE ARCHETYPES
  · MECHINATION (Earth/Mecha) — combo. Every part searches the next
    one by CATEGORY, not by name, so the chain never runs the same way
    twice. Feed the pieces to something bigger
  · SLEIGHTWIND (Wind/Demon) — these monsters are discarded from your
    HAND during your opponent's turn to stop an attack, negate an
    effect or bounce a monster. They never need to reach the field
  · KINDLEKIN (Fire/Beast) — six Level 1 beasts that pull each other
    out for free. The count is the point, and the count is what the
    Reliquaries ask for. The last one burns everything but BEAST
  · MANACLE (Dark/Myth) — takes your opponent's Mana. Coupled effects
    let you choose: a little right now, or cut their NEXT turn short
    before it even starts
  · SACRILEGION (Light/Dragon) — its Sacraments demand monsters from
    BOTH sides of the field. The summon itself is the removal

CARDS CAN CARRY TWO INFUSED EFFECTS
  · Until now every card had at most one. Ten of the new ones have two
    — usually a Coupled upgrade plus an independent Standalone
  · Coupled effects share one use per turn with the effect above them:
    the free version OR the expensive one, never both. Standalone
    effects run alongside everything else

CHOOSE YOUR FIRST DECK
  · New duelists pick one of five ready-made decks before the main
    menu, and keep every card in it. Click a deck to read what it does
    and look through the whole list, card by card, before deciding
  · Everyone who already has an account gets the choice on their next
    start. One deck per account
  · TIDE AND TEMPEST is the gentle one. If you have never played a
    card game like this, start there

FINISHES REACH THE TABLE
  · A Glossy card in your deck is now Glossy in the duel. Until now a
    finish only ever existed while opening a pack — the copy on the
    table had no finish at all, because nothing carried it there. It
    now travels the whole way, from the deck list to the board, solo
    and online alike
  · It belongs to the copy, not to the card. Own three Plain and one
    Static and only the one you built in will flicker. Which one you
    built in is decided by the row you added it from
  · Your opponent sees yours and you see theirs. Face-down cards show
    nothing — a shimmering back would give away what is lying there

DECK BUILDER
  · Clicking a row shows that copy on the right. It used to show the
    plain version no matter which row you clicked, so a Rainbow you
    owned was something you could never actually look at
  · A column beside the preview switches between all four finishes.
    What you own is lit and counted, the rest stays dimmed but can
    still be viewed — worth knowing before you open a pack
  · The highlighted row is now the copy on display. With three
    finishes all three rows used to light up at once

FIXED
  · The build number in the shop was typed into the screen and had
    been wrong for a while. It reads itself now

0.1.0h · CARDS THAT MOVE
────────────────────────────────

ONLINE
  · Cards move again. Playing from your hand to the field teleported the
    card instead of flying it there — the server recorded the movement,
    the client had no case for it and threw it away. Same for the moment
    that remembers where a card started; without it a flight has no
    starting point and does nothing at all
  · You can see when your opponent is deciding. A prompt on their side
    left you looking at a board that would not move, with no way to tell
    thinking apart from frozen. It now says what they are doing

NO MORE SILENT LOOPS
  · An answer the server rejected was re-sent forever, and not one line
    of it reached any log. That is indistinguishable from a freeze. After
    three rejections the request resolves neutrally and says why

0.1.0g · ONE COIN TOSS
  · There were two coin tosses in the code — a whole screen for solo and
    an overlay for online. You only ever saw one, but they were two
    things to keep in step, and they had already drifted apart
  · The overlay is now the only one, and it got everything the screen
    had: the darkened board, rising embers, the caption during the
    flight, the camera easing in and back out on impact, and the verdict
    banner. Solo and online show the same flip
  · The coin toss screen is gone. Duels load straight to the board

ONE WAY TO DUEL
────────────────────────────────

THE REAL COIN TOSS, ONLINE TOO
  · Online duels flipped a plain gold square. They now get the actual
    coin — your equipped one, if you have one — rising, tumbling, landing
    with a squash and a ring of dust, and stopping on the winning face
  · It runs as an overlay rather than its own screen, because online the
    server decides the toss mid-duel and there is no room to push a whole
    scene in front of it. Solo and online now show the same flip

ONLINE DUELS LOOK LIKE SOLO ONES NOW
  · Attacks, destructions and Extra Deck summons had no animation online.
    One cause behind all three: the board was updated first and the
    animations played afterwards, so a card shattered on a table it had
    already left, and a Reliquary stood in its zone before the vault
    opened. Events are now sent at the seam where they belong — before
    the change they describe
  · The Extra Deck monster appears only once the vault animation ends

NO MORE DEAD DUELS
  · A request the client could not read left both players staring at a
    board they could not touch — the server was waiting for an answer
    that would never come. Nothing hard-locks now: an unreadable request
    is answered, logged, and the duel goes on
  · The same for a targeting prompt whose targets the client did not
    know. That was the artifact lock

SEARCHING YOUR DECK WORKS AGAIN
  · A spell that fetches a monster from your Deck offered you nothing to
    pick. Your Deck is hidden information and is never sent card by card,
    so the client threw those candidates away and showed an empty window.
    It now builds them from what the server sends with the prompt

ALT-TAB
  · The game kept running when it lost focus. It used to freeze and then
    catch up in a rush, which is why an alt-tabbed opponent looked stuck

ONE WAY TO DUEL
  · Online duels ran on two completely different systems. Either the
    server computed the duel and both clients displayed it, or both
    clients computed it themselves in lockstep and only exchanged moves.
    Which one you got depended on whether you were signed in
  · Only the first was ever maintained. Every fix of the last while went
    past the second one, and nobody could tell which they were in
  · The lockstep path is gone. Every online duel is computed by the
    server. If a duel cannot start, you get told why instead of quietly
    dropping into something worse

BOTH PLAYERS SEE THE SEAL
  · Losing a duel now ends on the winner's victory seal, with their name
    under it. A seal is a signature on a win, and one only the winner
    ever sees is not a signature. It runs after the defeat sequence, not
    over it — both are skippable

ALSO
  · Extra Deck summons open the vault at the right pile instead of the
    middle of the screen — the two anchors were never wired up
  · Animations no longer go missing for cards the client had not seen
    yet, which is most of what comes out of an Extra Deck
  · Thorn Setting's spikes break out of the frame instead of notching
    into it. That silhouette is the whole point of the frame at 44 px

COSMETICS SHOP
  · The shop scrolls, and it shows everything. It was drawing the first
    twenty items and silently dropping the other thirteen
  · Filter by category: backs, mats, coins, frames, seals, titles

0.1.0f2 · BOTH SIDES OF THE TABLE
────────────────────────────────

  · You now see your opponent's cosmetics. Their card back sits on their
    face-down cards and their hand; it arrives with the match, before the
    first frame of the duel, so nothing pops in later
  · The duel mat is split down the middle — your mat on your half, theirs
    on theirs. Each is drawn at full field size and cropped, so the
    patterns still sit where they were designed to sit
  · The toss coin cannot be split, so one rule decides it: player A's coin
    flies. Always, no exceptions
  · News scrolls. The panel had a scroll area all along, but nothing in it
    accepted the mouse wheel, so the wheel went to the window behind it.
    There is a slim bar on the right now that shows how much is left

0.1.0f · WHAT THE WINNER LEAVES BEHIND
────────────────────────────────

VICTORY SEALS
  · Winning a duel now stamps your seal on the screen. There are five,
    and they differ in HOW they arrive: burned in, broken apart,
    opened outward, struck from above, eclipsed
  · Every seal finishes early and holds its last frame. That frame is
    the one worth a screenshot
  · Both players see it, so it is the loudest thing you can own

COSMETICS
  · The shop grew from 20 items to 33, and every one is now drawn as the
    thing it actually is — no more coloured swatches
  · Card backs (8), duel mats (6), toss coins (5), profile frames (6),
    victory seals (5), titles (3)
  · What you equip is finally visible: your card back on every face-down
    card, your mat under the duel, your coin in the opening toss, your
    frame around your portrait
  · Prices were re-laddered by rarity now that the full set is in. Most
    items got cheaper; nothing you already own changes

SHARPER
  · The level crest on cards was drawn at 44 px and blown up from there.
    It is now drawn four times larger with smooth edges instead of
    stair-steps — the same crest in the coin toss choice cards too
  · Every generated sprite lost its mipmaps and its compression. Mipmaps
    made the interface soft even at full size; compression put blocks in
    the gradients
  · The landing ring in the coin toss is drawn at twice the size

THE COIN
  · The back of the toss coin was a grey mush of overlapping spokes. It
    now reads as three clean bands — crest, ring, spokes — so you can
    tell which side is coming up mid-flip

0.1.0 · THE VAULT OPENS
────────────────────────────────

NEW CARDS
  · 40 new generic cards across two sets
  · New archetype: Fethaerbreese (Wind / Animal) — targeted negation on its bosses
  · The card pool now holds 223 cards across 13 archetypes

NEW MECHANICS
  · Banishment as a second graveyard — banish cards, then call them back
  · Mill: send cards from a Deck to the Graveyard. An empty Deck still only
    loses on the next draw
  · Take control of an enemy monster until the End Phase
  · Shuffle a card on the field back into its owner's Deck
  · Traps: set a Quick Spell and activate it on your opponent's turn
  · Copy a monster, copy its stats, taunt, position locks, targeting immunity,
    battle damage prevention, extra Normal Summons and summon locks

BANLIST
  · The banlist is now enforced when a deck is saved
  · Limits are shown directly on the card rows in the Deck Builder

DUEL
  · Attack targets are chosen on the field instead of in a list
  · "Up to" effects no longer force you to pick the maximum
  · Spells and Artifacts let you pick their zone
  · Special Summons from the hand let you choose Attack or Defense, always face-up
  · Duel log is readable again: dark ink on the parchment, card names coloured
    by card type

SOUND
  · Full sound pass: hover, click, typing, card draw, card placement,
    effect activation, hits, destruction and the coin toss
  · Separate volume slider for sound effects in the settings

FIXES
  · Effects targeting several cards now hit all of them, not just the first
  · Cards that state a summon position are no longer overridden
  · Coin toss: no more clipped headline, and the choice only appears
    if you actually won the toss
  · Opponent's hand is fully visible again
